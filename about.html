Who we are

A learning project,
built with clarity.

WU Connect is an independent prototype exploring how a money-services experience might be designed. It is not a bank, remittance provider or official Western Union product.

People before product

Good design starts with being clear about what happens next.

This prototype is a place to learn about interface design and connecting a website to a Python API. The sample screens are not connected to banking rails, payment providers or identity checks.

Our guardrails

What this prototype will
and won’t do.

01

Demo data only

We use sample amounts and destinations. Do not enter real personal or financial information.

02

No live payments

No money is sent or received. There are no live rates, fees or connected payment methods.

03

Independent identity

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Standard Bank or Western Union.

Before anything real

Authorisation, security and customer protection come first.

A live financial product would require appropriate regulatory advice, authorised payment integrations, security testing, privacy safeguards and operational support. None are established by this prototype.
